Long Channel HQ signs on as Halcyon Control now, every Derelict Sanctuary reads out a registry name when you pull its first cache, and there's more traffic on the radio the further out you get. Some manifest entries won't show up in the shop until you've earned them.
Supply Pallet Supply Drop's crate now includes a pallet of a random material alongside the fuel and hull kits. HQ reads the manifest out over the radio when it lands.
Derelict Sanctuaries Burnt-out company headquarters now drift out in the wild. Fly alongside one and press E to cut its salvage caches free for Stardust and local materials. Supply Drop New craftable blessing: call Halcyon Control on the long channel and requisition fuel and hull to your position. The crate holds station on its own beacon until you collect it. It never fades like ordinary pickups. 500s cooldown.

Stress Meter New Stress bar builds up while you sit still, faster still near debris or aliens. High Stress fills crystals faster but shrinks their Stardust payout. Craft Stress Vent to dump it all into an escape burst, or Steel Nerves to slow the buildup.

Supply Drop

Opens the long channel to Halcyon Control and requisitions a crate of fuel canisters, hull kits, and a pallet of a random material to your position. Unlike normal pickups, a called-in drop never fades. It holds station on its own beacon until you collect it, and an arrow points you back to it. / 500s cooldown

Gilbert Bullets

Permanent, purely cosmetic, replaces your bullets with mini "Gilberts", the size of debris. Stacks with whichever passive you have equipped. Requires 10x Gil Stone, cut out of the Frozen Gilberts ring, or out of The Settler, a million meters from home.

The Cheater's Cure

One-time cure for your Konami cheat, purges it completely and resets you to a normal run. Find The Cheater's Desolate biome and salvage 10x Cheater's Alloy (10% drop chance) to craft it.
